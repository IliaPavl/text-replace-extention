Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Replace Similar Text runs entirely on your computer. It does not send page content, replacement pairs, or settings to a server, and it does not use analytics or ads.

Data stored on your device

Folders, replacement pairs, matching rules, and interface language are saved in chrome.storage.local for this extension only. They are not synced to other devices unless you export a file yourself.

Access to websites

The extension can read visible text on pages you open so it can replace names and terms you configured. It does not collect browsing history and does not upload page text.

Permissions

storage — save your folders and settings on this device.

activeTab — Scan and Restore on the tab you are looking at.

scripting — inject the bundled local scripts into that tab if needed. No remote scripts.

Host access — replacements must work on whichever site you read on. Page content is not sent off the device.

Remote code

None. All JavaScript ships inside the extension package.
